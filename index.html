About the studio

A creative side quest in the heart of old Manila.

Lemon Lab Pottery Studio is a welcoming community space where curious beginners, makers, friends, and teams can explore clay through wheel throwing, handbuilding, and pottery painting.

Tucked inside Room 409 of the First United Building, the studio brings a contemporary, hands-on creative experience into one of Escolta’s enduring heritage spaces. Every piece begins with a lump of clay—and an invitation to notice the place where it is made.

The place behind the pottery

First United Building

also known as the Perez–Samanillo Building

Located on Escolta Street in Manila, the building dates to 1928 and is notable for its Art Deco architecture. Escolta has long held an important place in Manila’s commercial history; today, the building accommodates creative enterprises, artists, and cultural activities.

“From Heritage to Handcraft” connects Lemon Lab Pottery’s creative activities with the history of the First United (Perez–Samanillo) Building. Its Art Deco design and commercial heritage help visitors appreciate the place behind the pottery experience.
